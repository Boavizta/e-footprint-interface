# Content license for published modelings — DRAFT

Status: draft for Boavizta board validation · 2026-07-07
Companion to `spec.html` (public-model-sharing). This is the reuse regime shown to users at
publish time; it is not legal advice and should be reviewed by the board (and counsel if the
board wishes) before production activation.

## Proposal

Published modelings are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0)**.

Concretely, by publishing a modeling on e-footprint.boavizta.org, the author grants everyone the
right to view, copy, fork, adapt, and republish it — including commercially — provided they credit
the author (the public author name displayed on the modeling) and indicate whether changes were
made.

## Why CC BY 4.0

- **Forking is the point.** The feature's core promise is that anyone can consult and fork a
  published modeling. The license must make that unambiguous; CC BY is the simplest widely
  understood grant that does.
- **Attribution serves every persona.** Companies get credit for their transparency work,
  writers and researchers get citable authorship, and the platform's author-name display is
  exactly the attribution mechanism the license requires.
- **No share-alike condition on shared adaptations.** CC BY-SA would require adaptations that
  are shared publicly to use the same or a compatible license. It does **not** require private
  internal adaptations to be published. CC BY remains the proposal for flexibility in downstream
  sharing; the board should choose between that flexibility and keeping shared adaptations under
  ShareAlike. See [Creative Commons' explanation](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/ShareAlike_interpretation).
- **Not CC0.** Waiving attribution would undercut the citation and transparency use cases and
  remove the author's one durable form of recognition. Rejected.
- **Not ODbL.** Database-specific, share-alike, and legally heavier than needed for individual
  modelings published as documents. Rejected.
- **Standard license.** The [CC BY 4.0 deed and legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  provide the reuse license; the platform still needs its own publication terms and privacy notice.

## Rights and attribution to confirm

The publisher can license only rights they hold or may sublicense. The proposal does not relicense
third-party datasets, quoted material, or other content embedded in a modeling. Before activation,
review compatibility with the reference data that e-footprint includes, and identify any notices
or restrictions that must remain attached. CC BY does not override other applicable rights.

Public attribution should include the author, source link, license link, and an indication of
modification for a fork. A source URL alone is insufficient when its page has been deleted. The
spec review questions cover which citation metadata to retain in forks and exports. These points
follow the [CC BY 4.0 attribution conditions and notices](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Interaction with erasure (deliberate coherence)

Account or modeling deletion removes the content from the platform immediately — the author keeps
agency over their data and their share links. But CC licenses are irrevocable for copies already
made: forks published by others before the deletion remain published and legitimately so. The
terms of use must state both halves plainly:

> You can delete your published modelings at any time, which removes them and their share links
> from this site. Copies and forks made by others while your modeling was published remain
> licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## What the publisher warrants

At publish time the user confirms:

1. They have the right to publish the modeling's content (no confidential or third-party
   proprietary data).
2. They understand the modeling becomes public under CC BY 4.0 and that forks made while it is
   published are irrevocable.

## Surfaces where the license appears

- The publish dialog (checkbox-style acknowledgment, linking to the CC BY 4.0 deed).
- The public modeling page footer (license badge + author name = ready-made attribution).
- The terms-of-use page.

## Board decision needed

1. Confirm CC BY 4.0 (or direct an alternative).
2. Confirm the erasure/irrevocability wording above.
3. Confirm the proposed hosting terms: Boavizta hosts user publications without claiming their
   ownership. Have the applicable responsibilities, reporting process, and privacy/retention terms
   reviewed; an email report link alone does not establish a legal status or compliance.
4. Confirm compatibility with embedded third-party reference data and the attribution preserved
   in forks and downloads.
