When requests from different journey steps and usage patterns reach the same server, how can we attribute its footprint to each of them? There’s no easy answer: one has to compute the share of CPU and RAM load that is due to each step and journey, even down to the usage pattern.

That attribution logic has been one of e-footprint’s hardest parts to build, and is one of its most distinctive capabilities.

But the result is well worth it: the Sankey makes complex systems understandable at a glance. It shows where impact flows, and where the hotspots are.

🎥 Watch the video to see it in action on an example e-commerce model and visualize how different electricity carbon intensities shape use-phase impact in France and the US.
