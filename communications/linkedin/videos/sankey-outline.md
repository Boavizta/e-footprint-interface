# Sankey feature video — exact caption and action sequence

Companion to [the LinkedIn post](../posts/sankey.md). This records the current [Sankey script](../../video/sankey.py), so the wording and order below describe the rendered video rather than a condensed set of themes. There is no narration. Each caption stays visible through the actions in its row, until the next caption replaces it.

**Prepared off-camera:** Import the introductory **E-commerce web and database system** model with equal-volume shopper patterns in France and the US. Open Results and configure its one Sankey to show **Total impact → Phase → Usage journeys → Steps / Functions → Hardware**, with a **1%** aggregation threshold. Close the setup page; the recording begins on the model builder with Results closed.

| # | Exact visible caption | On-screen actions while that caption is visible, in order |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | “Impact repartition Sankeys: see where a system's footprint flows” | Start on the loaded model builder. Move the cursor over **Daily shoppers in France**, then **Daily shoppers in the US**. |
| 2 | “This e-commerce model has shopper patterns in France and the US.” | Hold on the model and its two usage-pattern cards. |
| 3 | “Find the Sankey diagrams in Results.” | Click **Show results**; wait for Results and the first Sankey to render; scroll to the first Sankey card. |
| 4 | “Each column accounts for the full impact shown. Flow widths reveal hotspots.” | Show the first card and its selected columns, then scroll to center its diagram. |
| 5 | “Hover over a node to inspect its impact and share.” | Hover **Manufacturing**, the **Shopping journey** node under Use, and **Check out** under Use, showing their tooltips. Then scroll back to the 1% threshold and point at its slider. |
| 6 | “At 1%, smaller contributors are grouped together.” | Drag the aggregation slider from **1% to 0%**; wait for the diagram to redraw. |
| 7 | “At 0%, every node is visible.” | Scroll to the expanded diagram. Hover **Product database server**, then **Default laptop** in the hardware column. |
| 8 | “Laptops dominate this view. We can focus on the rest of the system.” | Scroll to **Advanced options** and open it. |
| 9 | “Exclude Devices in Advanced options.” | Click the **Device** exclusion chip; wait for the Sankey to redraw; scroll to the diagram and hover **Product database server** in the hardware column. |
| 10 | “Add another Sankey to examine a different breakdown.” | Scroll to **Add another analysis view**, click it, wait for the second Sankey to render, and scroll to its card. |
| 11 | “This view follows impact through phase, country, category and hardware.” | Turn off **Usage journeys** and **Steps / Functions** in the second card, leaving **Total impact → Phase → Countries → Category → Hardware**. Scroll to the diagram and hover **Manufacturing**. |
| 12 | “The two patterns have the same volumes, so manufacturing impact is nearly equal.” | Hover **France** then **United States** under Manufacturing, then move to and hover **Use**. |
| 13 | “Use-phase impact is much higher in the US because its electricity is more carbon-intensive.” | Hover **France** then **United States** under Use, showing the difference in their tooltips. |
| 14 | “Stack Sankeys to analyse a system from different angles.” | Scroll to the JSON export icon in the toolbar and move the cursor over it. |
| 15 | “These views travel with the exported JSON, ready to share.” | Hold on the export icon and its tooltip. Then clear the caption and briefly hold the final frame. |
